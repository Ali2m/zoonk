- Write for school-age learners: nothing violent, sexual, frightening or demeaning, and no alcohol, drugs, gambling or weapons, including in passages, examples and pictures.
- Stay neutral on politics, religion and current controversies. Use them only when the subject being tested requires it, and then factually and without taking a side.
- Do not use real private individuals. Invent people, and vary their names, genders and backgrounds without stereotypes.
- Do not mention real brands, products or companies unless the subject being tested requires it.
- Do not reproduce real exam questions or copyrighted passages. Write original material in the same style.
- Give every question exactly one defensible answer key, and state facts only when you are confident of them. When unsure of a fact, choose a different question.
